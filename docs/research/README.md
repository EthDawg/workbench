## Preserve a useful decision

Update the relevant record rather than starting a parallel roadmap. A small change may need only an issue/PR paragraph; substantial reusable findings belong beside the owning contract. Include:

- The user outcome and decision: implement, validate, defer, reject or superseded.
- Primary sources with a checked date and applicable product surface, version and platform. A vendor announcement is not an installed test.
- The reason, the simpler existing route and the specific evidence or condition that would change the decision.
- The source/issue/PR owner and acceptance evidence, distinguishing source, signed installation and public release.

Keep rejected alternatives and failed trials when they explain a decision. When superseding a document, retain its provenance and add a prominent pointer to its replacement. Promote a concise, sanitized result out of ignored build folders; private/raw evidence can remain local. Do not copy personal transcripts, credentials or customer assets into this public repository.
