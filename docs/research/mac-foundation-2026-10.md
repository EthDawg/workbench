## Primary research and application

### Ownership audit behind the deeper cut

The requested refinement includes why capabilities are connected, not only how many controls are visible. Source inspection found:

- [Present's chooser](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/StageKit/DemoScenesView.swift#L268) embeds the full `PersonaLibraryView`, including creation/edit/group controls. Yet [placing a Persona](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/StageKit/DemoScenes.swift#L573) already creates an independent scene copy. This supports a selection-only relationship, not two editors. A still copy inside the shared window has a clear output reason; a separate overlay's receiver visibility depends on the sharing route.
- [Present's unrelated prompt/browser entries](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/PresentWorkspaceView.swift) and [Library's Chrome setup](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/DemoLibraryView.swift#L28) expose separate concepts. General text reuse belongs to Library. Browser retirement must also close [app-menu/shortcut routes](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/main.swift#L451) and [automatic `browser.enabled` startup](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/PresenterBridge.swift#L101). The fresh default shortcut is already disabled; upgraded saved bindings are a distinct acceptance case.
- [Scene sync](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/StageKit/MacSceneSync.swift#L215) is independent of phone-photo handoff. Its adapter also owns local migration and portable assets, and refreshes on activation. Removing the adapter would endanger the foundation; pausing cloud admission while retaining local storage/recovery is the bounded cut.
- Recovery cannot reuse general refresh without examining its effects: [SceneLibraryModel](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/SceneSyncKit/SceneLibraryModel.swift#L184) resumes automatic sends and assembles uploads; [CloudSceneSync](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/SceneSyncKit/CloudSceneSync.swift#L179) can establish a zone before fetching. - [Hand-cutout controls](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/StageKit/DemoScenesView.swift#L408) remain a decorative authoring system, even when folded into scene options. The target preserves rendering/export of old assets and retires new authoring.

These are selected product decisions supported by current reachability and ownership, not observed usability trial results. The existing surface registry excludes some page-local controls and cannot by itself prove that startup, extension commands or cloud refresh have stopped. Implementation must trace both visible and indirect admission paths.

## Options and decisions

Hard gates were preservation of saved work, useful operation without optional accounts/permissions, one owner per live operation, explicit external transmission, and testable closure. No weighted vendor score is needed when an option fails those gates.

| Option | Decision and reason | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rewrite the entire app / new cross-platform shell | Reject now: broad regression/migration exposure, no remedy for OS/policy limits | A bounded owner cannot meet a measured requirement after profiling and focused repair |
| Rename/group every verb under modes | Reject: new learning and migration without completing the jobs | Observed first-use tasks still fail after direct outcomes, labels and hierarchy are corrected |
| Recompose existing native owners and finish delivery | **Choose now:** preserves substantial working recovery and History foundations while correcting the reported friction | Native evidence demonstrates a specific owner needs replacement |
| Skin/layout-only cleanup | Insufficient: cannot repair permission dead ends, false success or loss of result context | Use styling only inside an otherwise complete tested journey |
| Depend on QuickTime, Apple Mirroring or another receiver as core | Reject: different account/control/audio/policy limits and extra operational steps | An approved target deployment repeatedly proves one route superior; still keep scope explicit |
| Add automatic rich-file agent/MCP integration | Defer: current manual file route can serve the job; new authority/return ownership unproven | Repeated observed friction plus a confined, versioned result contract and actual host validation |
| Paste external meeting transcripts | Defer: useful possible feature, but creates source/provenance/duration/audio semantics beyond copy-out | Repeated external transcript need is confirmed; implement a narrow importer preserving exact original and honest origin |
| Repair all historical speculative tickets | Reject as reset scope: it recreates sprawl | A named user outcome passes the same admission criteria; dormant work remains historical |

Confidence is high in reuse, manual delivery, permission boundaries and the need for artifact-bound acceptance. Exact visual layout can improve during native testing. The work-Mac phone cause, all real call routes and universal host attachment behavior remain unknown. No claim of “bug-free forever” is supportable; the chosen mechanism makes regressions and feature growth accountable at delivery.

## Durable contribution direction

The maintainer clarified that this work must constrain future contributions across UI, naming, CI/CD, marketing, updates, permissions and settings. The canonical [Value before scope](../workbench.md#value-before-scope) policy therefore asks contributors to infer and research the real problem behind exploratory prompts, prefer reliable value and subtraction, and justify the full cost of additions. It preserves explicit user decisions and leaves ordinary implementation judgment with contributors. This is a selected product policy, not a finding that every existing subsystem is bloated or that all new features are wrong.

Independent review challenged photo retirement, self-declared acceptance coverage, learnability evidence and stale handoff feedback. The brief now preserves unimported downloads and enabled-sync controls; gets required acceptance variants from version-controlled policy outside the receipt; requires a short uncoached task trial; and binds feedback to the exact attempt. These were checked against current source and issue snapshots. No source or native acceptance is inferred from reviewer agreement.

## Adversarial review and Read decision

Second review on 6 October 2026, before app implementation. Main remains `7989426`; spec PR #278 was open at `db9d89d` when challenged. Three bounded reviewers examined the strongest keep case, retirement dependencies and the plan's product/execution assumptions. The maintainer's new statement that he rarely listens to generated audio and does not expect to return for selection reading is direct target-user evidence. It is not population research or proof that every Read path fails.

**Read verdict: retire the owned TTS workflow, not just its page.** The strongest keep case is prepared Markdown/assistant-answer listening, seek/replay and reusable exported audio. Current source implements substantial parts of that case: [reading imports](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/ReadSelectionService.swift), [playback/generation](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/AppModel.swift#L1484) and [provider research](../model-providers.md#reading-voices). The earlier request for neural voices is real; later experience can supersede it. Neither measured render speed nor unjudged naturalness establishes an enjoyable recurring workflow.

[Apple's macOS 26 Read & Speak documentation](https://support.apple.com/en-au/guide/mac-help/mh27448/26/mac/26), checked for this review, covers selected-text speech, keyboard invocation, highlighting, rate, pause/resume, stop and sentence skipping. This is a sufficient simpler alternative for the ordinary listening proposition, not feature equivalence: it does not establish Workbench's syntax-prepared/exportable artifact flow or identical selection/privacy behavior. Apple may speak available window text when nothing is selected. No native listening benchmark was performed here.

[PR #267](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/pull/267) is still open and genuinely improves selection start, draft preservation, focus and consistent Stop. Its author explicitly leaves several real-app/VoiceOver/shortcut paths unverified. Those are evidence limits, not the reason for retirement: even fixing them all would not establish demand. Hold its Read-only scope from the batch and preserve the work; independently useful shared fixes can be evaluated separately.

**Dissent considered:** one reviewer proposed retaining Read only as a contextual action, with no further provider investment. Rejected as the chosen target: it retains the same rendering, draft, provider, lifecycle, privacy and quality obligations while merely removing a prominent entry. No established repeated listening job presently justifies that continuing surface. Keeping old text and exported audio does not require keeping speech generation. Reconsider only for observed repeated use where the native route leaves material friction or an unmet accessibility requirement, with same-material quality and complete-use evidence. Another model, screenshot or nearly ready PR is insufficient.

Source checks exposed important removal hazards: [SavedState](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/Core.swift#L10) shares reading fields with Dictate/history; [Library resources](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/DemoLibrary.swift#L86) limit prompt text below Read's import limit; [release validation](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/scripts/release/preview.py#L79) currently requires the Read Service; [update admission](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/blob/7989426b41f84afaf8e38bf57ae4b2a68935aacb/Sources/LocalVoice/main.swift#L330) groups reading with still-needed prompt insertion. The spec therefore preserves the exact draft through one ordinary Library file, keeps compatible state, removes bundle/runtime admission together and retains shared recognition/audio owners. `Readback` is Snap & Talk, not Read. This is why removal needs a bounded implementation contract rather than deleting files by name.

Confidence is high in retiring Read for the focused offering and in the dependency boundaries; broader adoption of retained tools remains an explicit hypothesis. Advanced Persona, Library and provider expansion are frozen, not automatically deleted without known use/preservation evidence. This review changed the spec; it did not execute removal or certify a new app build.

### Screenshot judgment and platform research

The supplied Ghost Pepper images communicate what a permission is for, visible model progress and where the menu-bar app lives. Those are useful patterns. They do **not** establish actual permission enforcement, cancellation, successful audio routing or repeated value. Workbench should not copy a universal checklist, require Accessibility before manual paste, download optional models at startup, enumerate every capability in a completion screen or say “All set” while only some routes are ready. The selected design is contextual first use and observable task success. No artwork or identifying screenshot details are needed in the public repository.

Primary references checked for this audit:

- [Apple HIG: Privacy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy/) places permission requests in the context that explains their need. This supports action-specific requests, not a launch checklist. The search extract was readable; the full page requires JavaScript, so no additional full-page claims are made here.
- [Sparkle distribution documentation](https://sparkle-project.org/documentation/#4-distributing-your-app) warns that read-only/translocated applications may not notify by default when updates cannot proceed. That supports explicit recovery in the existing update experience; it does not imply Workbench lacks all error handling or needs a different distribution format.
