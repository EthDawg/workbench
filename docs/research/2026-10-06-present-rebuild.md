# Present rebuild: decisions ledger

Recorded 6 October 2026 with the Present rebuild ([issue #276](https://github.com/Ship-Work/workbench/issues/276)). This ledger keeps what the rebuild removed or left out, why, and what would bring each back, so a later change can reverse a decision on evidence instead of rediscovering it. [Phone presenting](../phone-presenting.md) owns the behaviour that shipped. The [visual contract](../../site/handbook/contract.json) keeps the retired capabilities and names this file in their evidence.

## Evidence

The trigger was a production failure on a managed work Mac on 6 October 2026. The phone never appeared in Present. iPhone Mirroring asked for an Apple Account, and QuickTime Player could not see the phone either, so the Mac itself was not receiving it. Workbench could not say which step had failed.

The maintainer's asks that day, as themes:

- Offer one or two of the best, most relevant ways to connect or mirror a phone, instead of a route guide of nested choices.
- Show the phone in the desktop app before Present is pressed, so readiness is visible before a call starts.
- Keep the floating toolbar and the menu-bar panel consistent with each other, without adding controls.
- Treat the animated background and iPhone photo sync as distractions: neither ever worked in practice.

The rebuild answers with one owner for the phone's state (`PhoneLink`), one capture shared by the Present page and the stage, one help sheet and fewer doors. The ledger is what that answer set aside.
