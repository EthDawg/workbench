# Mac experience: candidate release notes

Prepared for the next accepted Mac release. These are draft notes; no new public version, archive or update feed has been published by this change.

## Ready when you are

- **Start with an idea.** Home puts Dictate, Snap and Snap & Talk up front. Continue a loaded explanation from its card, or pick up a recent transcript or image in one place.
- **A little more room to create.** Collapse the sidebar to tidy icons, with names on hover. A short welcome settles into “Let’s make something. ✨”.
- **Put yourself in the picture.** Take or choose a photo for your local Me profile, adjust its appearance, then use it as a Persona when you present. Your previous scene artwork stays intact.
- **Small controls, clear actions.** The black floating pill opens into white icons, with a grey hover target and a shortcut hint. Stop, Pause and Resume follow the work you are doing.
- **See what your microphone hears.** The recording trace responds to softer input and follows syllables promptly. The Persona outline stays calmer for your audience and recedes between phrases.
- **Get settled on your Mac.** The download page has a direct download, matching release notes and three short setup steps, plus help when an update finds the app still in Downloads.

## Acceptance boundary

Use the integration verification record for test results and native limits. Publish these notes with the exact accepted signed archive and matching update feed only through the shared release workflow.
