## Release notes

# Workbench 2.5.0

A Home worth opening, your phone on a clean stage, and a way to tell us when something goes wrong.

- **Home shows what's yours.** Your latest meetings with the follow-up you prepared, your Snap & Talk decks (a sample deck shows until you build one), what this Mac lets Workbench use, and the keys worth learning next. Set up a permission from the same card; Workbench asks only when you choose to.
- **Learn your keys.** Your keys shows your shortcuts on a keyboard and rings the next ones worth learning. Practice one, change it, or put Snap on ⌥G in one click. A key counts as learned once you've practised it or used it for real.
- **Present with your phone.** Plug in your iPhone with a cable and it appears in the preview before you press Present. One line says what is happening and the one next step. If the phone doesn't appear, Can't see your phone? walks through the checks.
- **Report a problem.** Choose Help › Report a Problem…, say what happened, and add a screenshot or a short voice note if it helps. The report goes privately to the Workbench team, and its receipt says when it was received. The same button sits beside a Dictate or Snap problem.
- **Every page, one way.** Dictate, Meetings, Snap, Snap & Talk, Draw, History, Library, Persona and Settings share one set of cards, notes and a single primary action. A problem looks the same on every page, with its fix beside it.
- **The Mac's own menus.** Hide Others, Show All, Zoom, Bring All to Front and Redo are where you expect them, and ⌘M minimises the window. Workbench reopens where you left it, and opening at login keeps it in the menu bar.

Library's From iPhone section and the photo handoff settings are gone; they never worked in the public app. Photos already saved on this Mac are untouched.

Choose Settings › Workbench updates when you're ready. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.
