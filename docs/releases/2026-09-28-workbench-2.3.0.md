Published as [Workbench 2.3.0](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/releases/tag/v2.3.0), build `20260928035156`, from main source `63922860adf2bb918d6a93d9bab79183f10c88fc`. The signed production feed and matching website record promote those same immutable bytes together.

## What it contains

Everything merged to main after the September 27 update (`f2b8dfa`), each after CI passed on the then-latest main:

- [#117](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/117) React to my voice, a ring around the shown persona that moves as the presenter speaks. Off by default; the microphone runs only while the ring is on and its persona shows, and nothing is recorded. Native evidence is in [the verification record](../verification/2026-09-28-persona-voice-ring.md).
- [#129](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/129) Read aloud starts at once, highlights the spoken word and lists voices by accent and quality.
- [#116](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/116) Snaps are found by the text in their image, and organising proposes visual repeats.
- [#123](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/123) Import Desktop screenshots into Snap History, and [#141](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/141) the Snap page's note now says captures save to Snap History, not the Desktop.
- [#118](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/118) Meeting transcripts are labelled You and Others.
- [#115](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/115) Suggest details runs on this Mac first, with an assistant as a choice.
- [#120](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/120) Handoff skills ship as a built-in pack, including meeting notes and follow-up.
- [#124](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/124), [#125](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/125), [#132](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/132) and [#145](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/pull/145): the Grammar, surface registry check, documentation and version. No other shipping behaviour.

No storage schema migration is added. Stable and Preview keep separate data.

## Evidence

`scripts/release/release.py` packaged the clean main commit above after its regressions passed on this Mac with no Workbench running: 194 Swift package tests, the Stage suite (174 tests, 3,446 assertions), 64 browser-extension tests, every Python suite, the surface registry (327 entries) and 85 Snap checks. It then ran the configured self-checks on the signed component and the final extracted archive (core, input, self-test transcription round trip, presenter, readback, packaged resources, readback pack, transcript handoff with 129 contract and 11 runner checks, and the reading service). Apple accepted notarization submission `6b1b5288-c8db-4b24-bcc8-809f6c59c025`; the ticket is stapled and Gatekeeper accepts the final app. `publish_update.py` rechecked both Sparkle signatures and the notarized app, uploaded a draft, read it back, published, and matched the unauthenticated public download against the digest above.

Before packaging, the same changes ran in a Developer ID signed Preview on this Mac: every packaged self-check passed, and React to my voice passed its real-microphone check (its record is linked above). Independent reviews: Fable and Codex reviewed the queued surface work that is not in this release; #117 was reviewed by its owner session and by the release owner.

## Not verified

The in-app checks in [issue #140](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/issues/140) were not run by a person: Snap text search on a large library, You and Others on a real call, Suggest details and meeting notes with a real connected assistant, Desktop screenshot import with real screenshots, and Read aloud on real speakers with VoiceOver. React to my voice was checked with a synthetic sentence through the speakers, not a live voice, a Bluetooth headset or a meeting receiver's view. An old-to-new updater installation and a browser-downloaded first installation on another Mac remain unverified.
