## Release notes

# Workbench 2.4.1

This update makes History easier to work through and gives Persona a ring that reacts to your voice.

- **Select captures without repeating the search.** History reuses unchanged search results while you select items. Editing their details still refreshes the results.
- **Keep one saved screenshot when cleanup fails.** If macOS cannot move an imported screenshot to Trash, Workbench reuses the saved Snap and waits before trying again. It checks the saved original before removing the source.
- **A ring that moves as you speak.** Turn on **React to my voice** in Persona and a ring of dots around the shown persona rises into bars as you talk. Bars stay in place, and different sounds raise different parts of the ring, so it reads as your voice rather than a volume meter. Choose its colour beside the switch, or under **Persona Overlay › Voice Colour**, with the same swatches and picker as Draw's ink; it starts mint. The microphone is used only while that persona shows, and nothing is recorded.
- **A voice on the toolbar.** While you dictate, the floating toolbar's recording trace is seven bars, tallest in the middle, that swell outward with each syllable and rest as dots when you pause.
- **Still, if you prefer.** With Reduce Motion on, both stay still and only brighten.

Choose Settings › Workbench updates when you're ready. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.

## Known limits

The ring and trace were chosen from rendered clips driven by the Mac's own voices and checked for timing with those voices (lit within 17 ms, at rest within 283 ms). A real voice at the desk, a Bluetooth headset, external displays and a meeting receiver's view of the shared screen have not been newly certified. Everything 2.4.0 listed as not newly certified still stands.

The screenshot recovery and History selection changes passed synthetic regression checks. Native acceptance on the final signed Stable package must still be recorded before this draft is promoted.
