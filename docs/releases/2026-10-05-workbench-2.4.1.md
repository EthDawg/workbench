## Release notes

# Workbench 2.4.1

Follow your words as you speak, keep conversations together and move more easily from saved work to the next step.

- **See words while you speak.** Meetings and Dictate show a live transcript with local Parakeet v2. Compatible text fields can receive live dictation directly; other fields keep the existing delivery when you finish. The floating pill stays compact.
- **Keep a conversation together.** Start a detected supported call in one click, pause and resume the same recording, and see when audio is reconnecting. Supported calls offer a cancellable finish countdown after their app stops using audio; silence alone never ends a recording.
- **Prepare what comes next.** A saved meeting keeps its transcript visible with review and follow-up preparation beside it. Send the conversation to your chosen destination for decisions, commitments and a useful draft; nothing is sent automatically.
- **Keep saved work dependable.** History reuses unchanged search results while you select captures. Screenshot imports avoid duplicate Snaps after a failed Trash move, and unreadable Dictate preferences are preserved before replacement.
- **See your voice in the controls.** Dictation has a responsive seven-bar trace, and Persona's optional voice ring has a colour you choose. Reduce Motion keeps both still. Persona uses its microphone only while shown and records nothing.

Choose Settings › Workbench updates when you're ready. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.

## Known limits

The ring and trace were chosen from rendered clips driven by the Mac's own voices and checked for timing with those voices (lit within 17 ms, at rest within 283 ms). A real voice at the desk, a Bluetooth headset, external displays and a meeting receiver's view of the shared screen have not been newly certified. Everything 2.4.0 listed as not newly certified still stands.

The screenshot recovery, History selection, preference recovery and live voice changes have synthetic regression evidence. Actual receiving-app compatibility, relayed phone calls, physical headphone switching and long meetings remain separate acceptance checks. Live delivery requires compatible Accessibility text operations. Call-end detection uses audio activity, not a provider's call-end API; a provider that keeps audio running still needs Finish. You/Others are capture channels, not identified speakers.

New live meetings use format 2 and preserve the prior format-1 manifest and original tracks. Older binaries cannot read format-2 sessions; retain the recording folders when rolling back. Preference recovery copies remain in the same edition's preference domain and are not independent backups.

Native acceptance on the final signed Stable package must still be recorded before this draft is promoted. No individual PR or prior Preview installation is proof of the combined released package.
