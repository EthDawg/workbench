# Workbench 2.4.0

Status: **published Stable**. [Download and release notes](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/releases/tag/v2.4.0). The [production record](../../site/updates/production.json) binds the public archive to its signed update feed. Installed update evidence and remaining native follow-ups are recorded in [#134](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench/issues/134).

Keep dictation, capture and presentation controls close to the work you are doing.

- **A clearer floating toolbar.** The three-dot menu is gone. Each tool shows its next action, and Switch tool keeps running jobs and their controls together.
- **A live Persona camera bubble.** Show a mirrored webcam over your other apps, with Start, Hide, Show again and End controls. Nothing is recorded by the bubble; camera availability depends on other apps using the device.
- **Pick up unfinished captures.** Snap & Talk brings capture, narration and review into one workspace. Review unfinished Snap reopens its draft directly, and a narration that hears nothing keeps the screenshot.
- **More useful speech controls.** Meetings has its own page with Pause and Stop & transcribe. Sublime dictation uses one guarded paste and keeps your words copied; a changed or unreadable destination retains the copied fallback.
- **Consistent everyday actions.** Timer uses the same Start, Pause, Resume and Stop controls across surfaces. Stop drawing closes a whiteboard while keeping its ink, and microphone-refusal messages offer the relevant settings shortcut.

Choose Settings → Workbench updates when you are ready. Your saved work stays in this edition’s library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.
