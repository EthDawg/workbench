## Release notes

# Workbench 2.4.0

The floating toolbar now shows the next step in every tool, with nothing hidden behind a menu, and the desktop app is organised around what you are doing.

- **Nothing hidden on the toolbar.** The ⋯ menu is gone. Each tool shows its next step and only the controls that help right now: Region, Window and Screen for Snap, Review beside a Snap & Talk session, Tools for Draw, Prompts and View for Present, and your personas and camera for Persona. **Switch tool** lists every running job with its own Stop, Pause or Cancel, so a presentation and a persona are managed from one place. A paused reading no longer takes over the other tools.
- **Put it where you want it.** Drag the toolbar anywhere. Near an edge it attaches, as a row along the top or bottom or a column at the sides, and it always stays on screen. Position… and Keep open are in Settings › General and on the toolbar's right-click.
- **Appear live.** Persona's camera bubble shows a mirrored webcam over your other apps. Start, hide or end it from the toolbar, Home or the menu bar. Nothing is recorded, saved or streamed.
- **Dictation that lands.** Dictating into Sublime Text pastes once and keeps your words copied, and rich-text fields take text more reliably. If the microphone is off, **Microphone Settings…** is one click away where you see the message.
- **Meetings has its own page.** Transcribe a meeting or call from the sidebar or Home, and play a meeting's original recording from History.
- **Snap & Talk in one workspace.** Capture, narrate and review in one place. A narration that hears nothing says so at the toolbar, and an unfinished Snap is kept when you close the editor.
- **Draw and Present tidied.** Stop drawing also closes a whiteboard, Draw starts with the tool you chose, and Present's View menu holds only actions.
- **One Timer.** The timer has one name and one set of words on every surface, and the speech model's download shows wherever readiness does.

Choose Settings › Workbench updates when you're ready. Your saved work stays in this edition's library.

First download: unzip Workbench, move it to Applications in your home folder, then open it there. When replacing an existing copy, quit it and use the same Applications folder.
