# Mobile utility decisions

For the proposed phone-screenshot workflow, portable agent handoff and selected team-capture reuse, see [Snap & Talk on mobile](research/snap-and-talk-mobile.md). That research recommends Mac image import and an experiment combining existing phone presentation, scene backgrounds and annotation, followed by a measured mobile capture experiment; it does not change the current mobile capability record.

Reviewed 13 September 2026. This is a bounded comparison of current vendor/Apple documentation and a sample of publicly surfaced US App Store reviews and forum reports. It is **not** an exhaustive export of every review, a representative sentiment survey or a hands-on benchmark of every competitor. Review dates matter: a past complaint does not establish a current defect. The native Workbench test record is separate in [iOS Preview](ios-preview.md).

## What changes the product decision

Free local dictation is already available on iPhone. The useful advantage to pursue is recoverability and control: keep the recording and original words, make model readiness explicit, and provide a deliberate route to Copy, Share or Read aloud. For images, preserve a composition when replacing one named layer. For wallpaper, finish an independent crop/export job and hand installation to Apple.

| Category | What the baseline already does well | Ranked opportunities for Workbench |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dictation | Apple types into ordinary fields. Wispr, Superwhisper and VoiceInk add keyboard workflows, history, formatting and models. | **1. Recoverable capture and explicit delivery.** 2. Clear device/language/model readiness. 3. Conservative cleanup and approved vocabulary. |
| Reading | Apple Read & Speak supplies screen/selection reading, voices, rate and highlighting. Voice Dream and Speechify offer broader document libraries. | **1. Dependable local listen/pause/resume.** 2. A short, comprehensible installed-voice chooser. 3. Paragraph navigation and a useful retained reading position. |
| Annotation | Apple Markup and PencilKit cover native ink; Annotable adds specialised explanation tools. | **1. Screenshot → readable mark → Share, preserving the original.** 2. Verified solid redaction. 3. Resumable Pencil work and focused callouts. |
| Presentation preparation | Canva/Photoroom supply rich image composition; Keynote provides presentation and external-display controls. | **1. Replace one layer without disturbing the others.** 2. A clean audience view with deliberate End. 3. Explicit portrait/landscape outputs with stable placement. |
| Wallpaper | Apple owns Lock/Home customisation, blur and Photo Shuffle; Vellum/Walli compete through curation and breadth. | **1. Device-fit preview and export.** 2. Personal favourites. 3. Optional matching Home/Lock variants. |
| Saved items | Yoink provides a temporary shelf and many native entry points; Anybox specialises in searchable bookmarks and archiving. | **1. Find and resume work without guessing what was saved.** 2. A reliable incoming Share extension. 3. Deliberate portable content exchange, then sync only with conflict/recovery evidence. |

These rankings are our inference from the product goals and evidence below, not vendor statements. The first mobile increment implements one coherent native baseline across the existing jobs. It does not attempt to replace every specialist feature listed here.

## Voice and reading evidence

- **Apple:** [Dictation](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/dictate-text-iph2c0651d2/26/ios/26) already mixes voice with typing and supplies punctuation/line-break commands. [Read & Speak](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/hear-whats-on-the-screen-or-typed-iph96b214f0/26/ios/26) is the baseline to beat for selection/screen reading. An in-app utility cannot claim to be a system keyboard substitute merely because it recognises speech.
- **Wispr Flow:** the [3 September keyboard setup guide](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/7453988911-set-up-the-flow-keyboard-on-iphone) describes internet and Full Access requirements and a manual return to the prior app on newer iOS. Its [Notes guide](https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/3529886556-using-notes-in-wispr-flow-for-ios) describes retaining a note when backgrounding stops work. The lesson is to make interruption recovery visible, not to promise effortless cross-app input without testing the real handoff.
- **Superwhisper:** the [iOS listing and release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/superwhisper-ai-dictation/id6471464415) describe local/cloud choices, history and modes; August 2026 notes made Whisper models free. [Sample reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/superwhisper-ai-dictation/id6471464415?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews) mention clipboard interference and switching friction. We therefore avoid automatic clipboard reads and a custom keyboard in this Preview.
- **VoiceInk:** [the iOS product page](https://tryvoiceink.com/ios) and [listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voiceink-ai-dictation/id6751431158) establish a real mobile product, local Parakeet options, vocabulary and shared-audio import. Free beta is not a permanent free-price claim. [Dated reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voiceink-ai-dictation/id6751431158?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews) report language/model confusion and return-to-app friction. A readiness label must reflect installed assets and actual supported locale.
- **Speechify:** [iOS features](https://speechify.com/ios/) and [website import instructions](https://speechify.com/blog/listen-to-websites-in-the-app/) emphasise a broad listening library. Downloaded converted audio does not prove on-device generation. [Reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/speechify-text-to-speech-pdf/id1209815023?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews) include both useful listening outcomes and subscription/connectivity concerns. Workbench begins with installed Apple voices and explicit text input.
- **Voice Dream:** [the listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voice-dream-natural-reader/id496177674) offers document listening, navigation and offline use. [Review examples](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/voice-dream-natural-reader/id496177674?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews) mention voice/rate discoverability and heat. They justify a clear chooser and a real device resource test; they do not establish current universal defects.

Apple's [SpeechAnalyzer introduction](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/277/) supports a system-managed on-device model strategy. The [permission documentation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/speech/asking-permission-to-use-speech-recognition) distinguishes SpeechAnalyzer from the legacy speech-server permission flow. Workbench records only after microphone consent and prepares downloadable speech assets through an explicit action. A new engine must pass the same recovery, cancellation and result contract; model-brand popularity is not sufficient.

## Visual evidence

[Apple Wallpaper](https://support.apple.com/en-ph/guide/iphone/iph3d267104/ios) already separates Home and Lock choices. [Vellum](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vellum-wallpapers/id1095068317) and [Walli](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/walli-cool-wallpapers-hd-4k/id1061097668) reinforce curation as a product strength. Workbench needs a few excellent starting images and reliable crop/export before a feed, accounts or catalogue scraping. Its synthetic Coast starter is clearly recorded in the design provenance.

[Apple Markup](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/119875), [PencilKit](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/pencilkit/pkcanvasview) and [Annotable](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/annotable-annotation-markup/id1099850421) establish how much native functionality is already available. The Preview keeps editable ink separate from the original and exports a flattened copy. Redaction is deferred until tests prove the exported pixels actually conceal content; translucent highlighting is not redaction.

[Photoroom's replacement instructions](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/13002391-replace-a-background-ios) and [template setup](https://help.photoroom.com/en/articles/12638867-use-your-own-image-file-as-a-background-on-a-template) distinguish backgrounds from replaceable subjects. A January 2025 [review](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/photoroom-ai-photo-editor/id1455009060) describes difficulty preserving one while replacing the other. [Canva's frame controls](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/using-frames-variantb/) demonstrate useful composition depth. We select four ordinary labels—Background, Image, Logo, Persona—and keep their state independent. A full design editor, generation service or brand-management system is unnecessary.

## Saved items and bridges

[Yoink's iOS product](https://eternalstorms.at/yoink/ios/) and [usage tips](https://eternalstorms.at/yoink/ios/tips/) show the value of temporarily keeping an item through a native workflow. [Anybox](https://anybox.app/) offers native search and Share entry points; [its getting-started guide](https://anybox.app/getting-started) is explicit about actions on a saved item. A [public forum report about sync timing](https://www.reddit.com/r/macapps/comments/1vtafbv/anybox_replacement_with_background_sync/) is an anecdote, not a validated bug; it illustrates why “saved here” and “available on the other device” need separate evidence.

Workbench's bridges remain user-chosen: read a transcript, mark a picture, reuse an original for wallpaper or a scene, export to Files/Photos/AirDrop. The app does not monitor every clipboard change or silently synchronise edits. A future Share extension must prove bounded imports and process-safe App Group writes, as required by [Apple's extension guidance](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/General/Conceptual/ExtensibilityPG/ExtensionScenarios.html).

## Platform constraints that shape the UI

1. **Input:** [custom keyboards](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/configuring-open-access-for-a-custom-keyboard) cannot acquire microphone access merely by enabling Full Access. Desktop global keys/paste are not portable iPhone controls.
2. **Overlays:** [Apple DTS](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/832639) explicitly says there is no public cross-workspace iPad Pencil overlay API. Our canvas is inside Workbench.
3. **Wallpaper:** [Live Photo wallpaper](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/120734) plays on wake, not as a persistent animated desktop. [24 Hour Wallpaper's setup](https://24hourwallpaper.com/24hoursetup/) uses a separately configured workflow. Export and native setup are honest first steps.
4. **Presenting:** [external-display scenes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/uikit/presenting-content-on-a-connected-display) can host owned content, but separate presenter controls require real cable/AirPlay testing. [Keynote](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/keynote-ipad/tana4da2681/ipados) documents these variations. This Preview's Show action is an in-app composition, not a verified remote receiver route.
5. **Meetings:** [Zoom](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0078476) says mobile browsers cannot initiate screen sharing; [Teams](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/teams/meetings/use-microsoft-teams-on-the-web) says its mobile web experience is unsupported. Use native meeting apps and verify their recipient view.
6. **Capture evolution:** Apple's [new mobile ScreenCaptureKit sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/screencapturekit/capturing-screen-content-on-ios) requires iOS 27+, beyond this stable iOS 26 target. It is not evidence that arbitrary browser overlays become possible. ReplayKit is a separate capture decision, not a prerequisite for static scene preparation.

## Critical review and the next evidence

Claude challenged five equal feature tabs, ambiguous cleanup, a shared asset graph and background microphone scope. We accepted two tabs, reused tested cleanup, independent derived edits and foreground-only capture. It recommended dropping all persona composition; we rejected that blanket cut because a bounded, already-established presentation job is useful. The mobile version limits layout instead of introducing blend modes or a general layer editor.

The first image experiment invented PDF support; the second added a misleading tagline about making images. Neither is in the implementation. Concept art informs spacing and hierarchy; the native screenshots and acceptance tests decide what exists.

Physical speech quality, model preparation time, memory/energy, interruptions, VoiceOver traversal, external displays and native meeting recipients still need explicit results. Do not call a simulator build evidence for those. The durable test/install record belongs in [iOS Preview](ios-preview.md).
