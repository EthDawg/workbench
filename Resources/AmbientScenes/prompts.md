## Window light clean plate

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Production asset, a beautiful exceptionally believable photographic desktop backdrop, wide landscape 16:9, no UI or text. A quiet modern Australian office in warm oak, cream plaster and linen. Fixed straight-on camera, level horizon, refined architectural photography with natural surfaces rather than glossy CGI. Composition: left half is elegant oak shelving, a cream wall and the corner of a work table at bottom left; centre has calm negative space suitable for placing a mobile-device overlay later. On the RIGHT half, a single enormous RECTANGULAR window directly facing the camera with horizontal top and bottom edges, dark thin framing, no diagonal perspective. Window opening runs approximately from x=58% to x=95%, y=12% to y=76% of whole image. Through it see CLEAN PALE BLUE CLOUDLESS SKY in upper 75% of window and a distant tree-lined Australian low-rise city skyline only along the bottom. No curtains, no objects crossing the sky, no plants in front of window, no humans, no lettering, no identifiable company, no watermarks. A single subtle ceramic cup is allowed on the foreground oak desk at bottom, outside window area. Professional but warm. This is a CLEAN PLATE for later accurately masked cloud animation; absolutely no clouds in sky, no motion blur, camera and all architecture remain still. Fine texture, soft balanced daylight, restrained saturation, not sterile showroom.

## Cloud detail

No sky, no horizon, no ground, no black or white backdrop, no checkerboard printed into pixels. Actual transparency everywhere except the clouds, with naturally feathered translucent edges. Soft broad daylight, airy off-white cloud texture with faint cool grey internal shading. Wide landscape cloud formation occupying the central horizontal band, leaving transparent margin on ALL four edges. Low contrast and delicate, not a storm, not cotton balls or cartoon. Photoreal atmospheric detail for a slow drifting sky layer in an office-window desktop scene. No text, no logos, no frame, no additional objects. Final alpha must be preserved.

## Refined leaf alpha extraction

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied eucalyptus branch image. KEEP the complete branch, twigs, leaves, gumnuts, shape and realistic colour. Remove ALL of the blurred green/brown background and haze. Output ONLY clean photographic leaves/twigs/gumnuts as a precisely cut out object on ACTUAL transparent alpha. Every gap BETWEEN leaves must be alpha=0, not green haze; the entire surrounding background must also be alpha=0. Preserve fine opaque leaf texture and narrow twig edges with proper antialiasing. No drop shadow, no glow, no translucent background halo, no checkerboard drawn into image. Keep overall wide composition and branch entering upper left. This must composite cleanly over a light blue sky without a green fog rectangle.
