Keep tools nearby

Click the Workbench icon in the Mac menu bar for Dictate, Read, Snap & Talk, Draw, Present, Persona Overlay and Timer, in that order. Click a shortcut label to change it in place; conflicts are explained before saving. Open Workbench, Settings and Shortcuts are always at the bottom.

The shared floating toolbar carries live Snap & Talk, Draw, Present and Persona Overlay controls. Hover over its tile to reveal the row; click the glyph for options. Choose Keep open to leave it visible. Changing the selected tool preserves other running work. Drag the glyph or status area to move it, or choose one of eight positions. Dictation and reading use compact active controls; their editors stay in Workbench.

For keyboard access, choose Window → Focus floating toolbar, then Space or Return opens its menu. Arrow keys choose an action. Escape closes the menu; Escape again returns focus to your previous app. The toolbar displays assigned shortcuts; use the menu panel or desktop Keyboard page to edit them.

  • Missing toolbar? Choose Window → Show floating toolbar in Workbench, or turn it on in Settings.
  • Missing menu-bar icon? Choose Window → Restore menu-bar icon. On a crowded Mac menu bar, the floating toolbar remains another way in.
  • Finished using the app window? Close it. Your tools keep running. Choose Quit Workbench to stop the app.

Dictate into a Mac text field

  1. Click where the words belong. Use your dictation shortcut or Dictate in the menu-bar panel.
  2. Speak, then Stop. Expand the recording controls for details or Cancel.
  3. Check the result. Workbench tells you whether the words were pasted or copied. If the destination changed, paste the copied text where you want it.

You can keep a device scene live and annotate while dictating. Done drawing keeps your marks; End scene ends only the scene. If text is ready while you are drawing, it is saved and waits to paste. Finish drawing or choose Copy now; Workbench checks the original Mac field again before any paste. Ordinary dictation and Snap & Talk narration share one microphone.

Dictate → Options puts Destination before Text Style and opens Recent Transcripts, which keeps the original and cleaned wording. Clipboard feedback stays below the action rows. Change shortcuts in Keyboard. If speech is not ready, open Models to complete setup.

Read something aloud

Open Read aloud, enter text and choose Listen. In apps that support macOS Services, select text and choose Services → Read Selection in Workbench. Review the imported text before listening.

Use the compact reading controls to pause, resume or stop. Skip through audio in Workbench without generating it again. Mac voices work without an account. Speko is an optional online provider configured separately in Models.

Capture a screen and explain it

If you move or delete a session folder, Workbench marks it Folder unavailable instead of showing stale sections. Use Locate folder… to reconnect a moved session, Check again after reconnecting a drive, or Remove from Recents to forget the entry. Removing a recent entry never deletes files.

  1. Create a Snap & Talk session. Choose a local folder in the app once.
  2. Capture the next moment. Use Snap & Talk on the floating toolbar or its shortcut. Workbench captures the display under your pointer, then records your explanation.
  3. Stop, then continue. The screenshot and narration stay together while the next moment is captured.
  4. Review when ready. Edit titles and narration, reorder sections, or use Hand off to prepare a slide-deck prompt for your chosen AI app.

The toolbar shows the session capture count and saving state, and hides during Workbench screen capture. Hand off copies a prompt and opens the chosen app; you decide when to share the session folder.

ServiceNow decks, ready for your team

  1. Install once. In Snap & Talk, choose Install ServiceNow pack. Workbench remembers ServiceNow as your new-session style.
  2. Capture and review. Create a new session and explain your screens. The complete deck design travels with that session.
  3. Hand off. Choose your AI app, grant access to the session folder and paste the prepared prompt. There are no separate skill or branding files for colleagues to email or upload.

Neutral is the initial default, and New session style lets you switch back. Existing sessions keep their own style even when you update Workbench or reinstall or remove a pack. The deck keeps every complete screenshot, concise visible copy and the exact edited narration in speaker notes. Portable fonts work without ServiceNow fonts; your agent still checks the finished slides. The pack includes generation helpers; the receiving agent may need its usual Python setup.

If a selected pack is unavailable, reinstall it from Snap & Talk or choose Neutral. Keep existing session folders and saved work. To roll this out, share this installation guide and the Workbench download with colleagues.

Prepare a scene, then present

  1. Add a scene. Choose a starter, your own backdrop or an editable scene copy.
  2. Make it yours. Double-click a title to rename it. Drag the device in the preview and add a customer logo if useful.
  3. Present. Choose a window for easy screen sharing, or full screen. Select the connected device when prompted.

In the scene list, use Command-click for separate items or Shift-click for a range. Delete or Backspace asks before removing the selected scenes. Drag rows to reorder; clear a search first. Return commits an inline name and Escape cancels it.

Workbench shows the phone's picture. Use the physical phone to tap, type and dictate. Connection and audio explains separate audio routes and Apple alternatives.

Use Present in the shared floating toolbar for device source, connection help, reconnect, proportions, background motion, window position and End. The menu-bar Present row reveals those live controls while a scene is running. Ending a scene preserves independent persona overlays and does not restore your desktop wallpaper.

The toolbar's Prompts picker uses Saved Resources, including favourites and Product/Persona tags. Click a destination field before opening it. Workbench inserts literal text progressively where supported, or pastes once and says so. Escape or Stop cancels; changing the field or selection stops insertion. It never submits the prompt or replays a partial result.

Choose a logo and see motion

Use the logo controls to search the web inside Workbench. Choose an image, review it and use it in the current scene. A website page address may not be an image: use a direct image URL, copy an image, or choose a saved file when needed.

Animated starters preview their motion in the scene editor. Pause for precise positioning. Reduce Motion, disabled animated-image playback and power constraints can keep a preview still; the controls explain why.

PNG exports are still images. An animated desktop is a separate, explicit action; quitting stops its motion and leaves the still picture. More about backdrops · Ambient starters.

Draw or show an overlay card

Choose Draw to mark a detail on your screen. Tools → Choose Colour opens the Mac colour picker and uses the same ink setting everywhere. Escape returns to the app underneath; clearing your marks is a separate action. Use Screenshot when you want Apple's capture tool.

A scene keeps a backdrop, device and optional artwork together. An overlay card floats above an existing browser or app. Use overlay cards when the demo stays in that app; they are optional when preparing a device scene.

Choose Arrange overlays to prepare several cards. Keep them locked while presenting so clicks reach the app underneath. Overlay card guide.

Use the Size slider in the shared toolbar's Persona Overlay menu to make a persona smaller or larger. With several cards, choose an overlay to target a copy, then use Add Overlay or Remove Selected. Remove affects that on-screen copy only. In the persona library, Add persona… creates or imports a card, Add / remove members… edits a group, and Remove saved persona… asks before removing a library entry. Hide keeps the saved persona.
