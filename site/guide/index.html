Use this guide for everyday Mac controls. The release notes identify the published package and its checks. Improvements follow the Mac quality gate; iPhone, iPad and Chrome extension development are paused. Existing saved work is preserved.

Keep tools nearby

Home opens Dictate, Meetings, Snap or Snap & Talk without starting anything. The sidebar groups Voice, Screen and Saved. Use History for finished work and Library for things you want to reuse. Your drafts and active work stay in place when you change pages.

Click the Workbench icon in the Mac menu bar for Dictate, Read, Snap, Snap & Talk, Draw, Present, Persona and Timer, in that order. Click a shortcut label to change it in place; conflicts are explained before saving. Open Workbench, Settings and Shortcuts are always at the bottom. Click another app or press Escape to close the panel; an unfinished shortcut change is cancelled and the previous shortcut stays. Wherever you start a Snap, Workbench opens the capture in the Snap editor; Escape in the selector takes you back to what you were doing, and Save & Copy returns you to your app, ready to paste.

Each menu row controls its own task. While you draw, Draw offers Stop drawing; a running scene still offers End presentation and a running timer still offers Stop timer. A start is dimmed when it would conflict with current work.

The shared floating toolbar carries live Snap & Talk, Draw, Present and Persona Overlay controls. At rest it is a quiet handle. Hover over it or click it to see your remembered tool and its next action. Recording, reading, processing and results still show a signal before you interact. The tool button on the row chooses another tool, and ⋯ holds that tool's options. Choose Keep open to leave the row visible. Changing the selected tool preserves other running work. Drag the mark, or the row by its tool button, action or empty space, to put it anywhere; release by a corner or edge guide to dock it there, or choose Position… for a dock or Reset position. Dictation, narration and reading run in the same toolbar: while they run, the mark shows it, and the row stops, pauses or resumes them, with Cancel under ⋯; their editors stay in Workbench.

For keyboard access, choose Window → Focus floating toolbar, then Space or Return on the tool button opens the list of tools; arrow keys choose one and Return selects it, and Tab reaches the action and ⋯. Escape closes the list; Escape again returns focus to your previous app. Hover over the action to see its assigned shortcut, including whether to hold or release it; use the menu panel or Settings → Keyboard to edit them.

  • Missing toolbar? Turn on Floating toolbar at the top of the menu-bar panel or in Settings → General, or choose Window → Show floating toolbar in Workbench.
  • Missing menu-bar icon? Choose Window → Restore menu-bar icon. On a crowded Mac menu bar, the floating toolbar remains another way in.
  • Finished using the app window? Close it. Your tools keep running. Choose Quit Workbench to stop the app.
  • Screen Recording off? Snap and Snap & Talk say so and link to System Settings. If Workbench already shows as allowed in System Settings, quit and reopen it. Your saved Snaps, sessions and narration keep working, and you can paste or import an image you already have. A managed Mac may keep screen capture off.

Dictate into a Mac text field

New to Workbench? Home guides your first dictation, even if you have already taken Snaps. Afterwards it shows your words and that they are also saved in History. Choose Skip for now to hide the guide; Show me a first dictation on Home, or in Settings › General, brings it back until you have dictated.

  1. Click where the words belong. Use your dictation shortcut or Dictate in the menu-bar panel.
  2. Speak, then Stop. The toolbar rests as a small mark with a red dot while you speak; your shortcut, or the toolbar's Stop, finishes, and its ⋯ holds Cancel.
  3. Check the result. Workbench tells you whether the words were pasted or copied. If the destination changed, paste the copied text where you want it. If nothing was heard, a short cue says so and the toolbar comes back by itself; a recording is kept on the Dictate page if you want to retry it. With Press & hold, keep holding your Dictate shortcut while you speak and release to finish; a quick tap shows that tip once. If a copy fails or a paste can't be confirmed, the menu-bar panel keeps saying so, with where your words are, until you copy those words again or dismiss it, even after you quit.

Automatic paste needs Accessibility approval. Until your Mac allows it, Workbench copies each transcript and says Copied. Paste with ⌘V. When you are ready, choose Set up automatic paste… in Dictate → Settings…. It shows the macOS request, or opens Privacy & Security › Accessibility in System Settings; your organisation may need to approve it.

You can keep a device scene live and annotate while dictating. Done drawing keeps your marks; End scene ends only the scene. If text is ready while you are drawing, it is saved and waits to paste. Finish drawing or choose Copy now; Workbench checks the original Mac field again before any paste. Ordinary dictation and Snap & Talk narration share one microphone.

Dictate → Settings… keeps Delivery, Text style, Activation and Your dictionary together. The main page keeps your transcript beside the microphone, with Copy text and More for cleanup, saving and corrections. History keeps completed transcripts with their original wording. Unfinished audio stays under Capture recovery on Dictate. Change shortcuts in Settings → Keyboard. If speech is not ready, open Settings → Models to complete setup.

Transcribe a meeting or call

  1. Open Meetings. Use Home or the Voice group in the sidebar.
  2. Choose what to record. Select the call app to capture other people through headphones, and include your microphone for your own voice. Microphone only captures what the Mac microphone hears.
  3. Start recording. Recording begins only when you choose Start. Choose Stop & transcribe when finished, or Stop & keep for later to retain the audio.
  4. Review the transcript. The completion card opens that recording’s transcript in History. From there, copy it or prepare meeting notes through Hand off.

Unfinished audio has Retry saved recording in Meetings. Recording options include Meeting or Call metadata and Sound settings. Optional meeting detection lives in Settings → General; an offer never starts recording. Calls that remain on your phone cannot be captured here.

Read something aloud

Open Read, enter text and choose Listen. In apps that support macOS Services, select text and choose Services → Read Selection in Workbench. Read aloud on a History transcript or a saved text opens it the same way. If Read already holds different text, choose Replace reading or Keep current; nothing plays until you choose Listen.

Reading starts almost at once, and a Mac voice highlights each word as it speaks. Voices show their accent and quality; while your language has only compact voices, Read names a free Premium voice you can add in System Settings. Use the compact reading controls to pause, resume or stop. Skip through audio in Workbench without generating it again. If a reading's audio can't be read, the reading stops, your text and voice stay, and Retry makes the audio again. Mac voices work without an account. Choose Voice & pace… to change the voice, speed or reading provider. Speko is optional and online; the page says which provider will receive the text before you choose Listen or Save audio.

Capture a screen and explain it

If you move or delete a session folder, Workbench marks it Folder unavailable instead of showing stale sections. Use Locate folder… to reconnect a moved session, Check again after reconnecting a drive, or Remove from Recents to forget the entry. Removing a recent entry never deletes files.

  1. Create a Snap & Talk session. Choose a local folder in the app once.
  2. Capture the next moment. Reveal the floating toolbar and choose Region, Window or Screen. Workbench captures your selection, then records your explanation. The shortcut captures the display under your pointer.
  3. Stop, then continue. Stop narration returns the toolbar to its capture choices. The screenshot and narration stay together while you capture the next moment. Escape in a selector cancels without recording.
  4. Review when ready. Review sits beside the capture controls. Edit titles and narration, reorder sections, or use Hand off to prepare a slide-deck prompt for your chosen AI app.

The toolbar shows the session capture count and saving state, and hides during Workbench screen capture. Hand off copies a prompt and opens the chosen app; you decide when to share the session folder.

Click an image to open the shared image workspace. Use Previous and Next, or the arrow keys, to browse; Fit, Actual size and pinch keep details readable. Choose Edit for a saved Snap, or Edit a copy for a session or Library image. Add text/comment boxes, arrows and marks, crop to slide ratios such as 16:9, rotate, and undo changes while seeing the result. Save & Copy keeps the finished image in History and on your clipboard. Originals stay intact, and image notes remain separate from text drawn on the image.

In Library → Resources, a supported local image offers Use in Present… and Use in Persona…. Review a backdrop for a saved scene, or create your first scene. For Persona, finish the portrait and choose Add persona. Cancel keeps the libraries unchanged. Your running presentation and shown persona stay as they are until you explicitly present or show the prepared work.

From a saved image, Save image to Library… saves the finished PNG in a folder you choose, then keeps a reference in Library. If only the file export succeeds, Workbench says where the file was saved and that Library was not updated.

Your team’s skills, scenes and personas

Private repository packs require Workbench 2.2 or later.

  1. Open your team’s pack link. Or open Library → Packs in Workbench and paste its private repository address. Connect the GitHub account your team has invited.
  2. Add the pack. Choose a compatible skill, or add your own scene or persona copy. Workbench checks for compatible updates and keeps installed content available offline.
  3. Capture, review and hand off. A new session keeps its complete skill and resources. Hand off copies a prompt and opens your chosen AI app; you decide when to share the folder and run it.

Your team’s repository owner installs Workbench Packs with read-only access to the pack repository once. Each colleague uses their own GitHub account. Share the pack link and this guide, without separate skill attachments.

Neutral remains available. Existing sessions and previously installed ServiceNow content keep their own complete design when Workbench or a pack changes. New company content comes from your team’s private pack; older public release history is unchanged. If a pack needs repair, remove and add it again in Library → Packs. Personal sessions, imported scenes and personas remain yours.

Use selected transcripts in a handoff

In History, tick the captures you want, choose Hand off and a compatible skill. Mark the words as My instructions when you dictated a prompt, or Reference material when the assistant should treat them as evidence. You can include a chosen Snap & Talk session. Original and cleaned wording stay together in the folder.

Prepare a scene, then present

  1. Add a scene. Choose a starter, your own backdrop or an editable scene copy.
  2. Make it yours. Double-click a title to rename it. Drag the device in the preview and add a customer logo if useful.
  3. Present. Choose a window for easy screen sharing, or full screen. Select the connected device when prompted.

In the scene list, use Command-click for separate items or Shift-click for a range. Delete or Backspace asks before removing the selected scenes. Drag rows to reorder; clear a search first. Return commits an inline name and Escape cancels it.

Workbench shows the phone's picture. Use the physical phone to tap, type and dictate. Connection and audio explains separate audio routes and Apple alternatives.

Use Present in the shared floating toolbar for device source, connection help, reconnect, proportions, background motion, window position and End. The menu-bar Present row reveals those live controls while a scene is running. Ending a scene preserves independent persona overlays and does not restore your desktop wallpaper.

The toolbar's Prompts picker lists the prompts saved in Library, favourites first. Type to search, or choose a Product or Persona to narrow the list. Click a destination field before opening it, then choose a prompt with a click, or with ↑ ↓ and Return. Workbench inserts literal text progressively where supported, or pastes once and says so. Without Accessibility approval, choose Copy prompt and paste with ⌘V. Escape closes the picker; Escape or Stop inserting cancels an insertion, and changing the field or selection stops it. It never submits the prompt or replays a partial result. To keep copied text as a prompt, open Library and press ⇧⌘S, or choose Save clipboard as prompt… from Add.

Choose a logo and see motion

Use the logo controls to search the web inside Workbench. Choose an image, review it and use it in the current scene. A website page address may not be an image: use a direct image URL, copy an image, or choose a saved file when needed.

Animated starters preview their motion in the scene editor. Pause for precise positioning. Reduce Motion, disabled animated-image playback and power constraints can keep a preview still; the controls explain why.

PNG exports are still images. An animated desktop is a separate, explicit action; quitting stops its motion and leaves the still picture. More about backdrops · Ambient starters.

Draw or show an overlay card

In Preview, open Me on Home to take or choose your profile photo. The camera opens inside your profile, with Cancel always available. Take a photo, review its appearance, then choose Use photo to save. If the camera cannot start, try again or choose a photo from a file.

Choose Draw to mark a detail on your screen. Tools → Choose Colour opens the Mac colour picker and uses the same ink setting everywhere. Escape returns to the app underneath; clearing your marks is a separate action. Use Screenshot when you want Apple's capture tool.

A scene keeps a backdrop, device and optional artwork together. An overlay card floats above an existing browser or app. Use overlay cards when the demo stays in that app; they are optional when preparing a device scene.

Choose Arrange overlays to prepare several cards. Keep them locked while presenting so clicks reach the app underneath. Bring the pointer near a card to move it from the handle on its top edge, or resize it from a corner or edge, even while it is locked. Overlay card guide.

Persona shows Selected, what you browse, apart from Shown, the card on screen. Size, position and lock sit beside Shown. Replace shown with and Update shown card change the shown card on purpose; browsing never does. Hide keeps the card, and Show again brings it back.

Each persona has an Appearance: Circle, Card or Original. A new portrait starts as a Circle; frame it in Edit appearance…. To change only the card on screen, choose Appearance in the Persona Overlay menu. It keeps its size, place and lock.

A new editable portrait is saved only when you choose Add persona. Cancel or Escape before then leaves your saved personas, selection and groups as they were.

Use the Size slider in the shared toolbar's Persona Overlay menu to make a persona smaller or larger. With several cards, choose an overlay to target a copy, then use Add Overlay or Remove Selected. Remove affects that on-screen copy only. In the persona library, Add persona… creates or imports a card, Add / remove members… edits a group, and Remove saved persona… asks before removing a library entry. Hide keeps the saved persona.

Show selected needs only that card's image. If another card can't load when you move to it, the shown card stays up, the Persona Overlay menu names the card and says why, and Next and Previous skip it. When no other card can show, they say so.

Turn on React to my voice in Persona or the Persona Overlay menu and a quiet outline around the shown persona brightens as you speak, so your audience can see who is talking. It uses the microphone only while that persona shows and records nothing. With several cards, the outline follows the selected overlay.

Help and updates

Included in this download

Workbench 2.3 lets React to my voice ring the shown persona as you speak, starts reading aloud almost at once, and finds a Snap by the words in its image. Organising a Snap selection proposes repeated captures of the same screen. Add image → Import Desktop screenshots… saves your Desktop screenshots in History, then moves the originals to the Trash. Open Snap for region, window and screen captures. Crop or annotate an image, then save it; Copy and handoff use that visible image while preserving your original.

Meeting and call transcripts label turns You and Others when you include the microphone. Suggest details fills in people, companies and tags on this Mac, and nothing is saved until you save. The built-in Meeting notes and follow-up skill turns a selected meeting transcript into notes and a follow-up message through your connected assistant.

Transcripts and Snaps share one search and saved selections in History. Add searchable person, company, purpose and tags without rewriting a transcript. In the common handoff review, check the selected material, its role and any recording limitations. Copy instructions works manually. Optional installed Codex and Claude Code connections return a draft and task receipt to History, beside what the task was made from; your provider's account and usage limits apply. File-producing skills continue through the complete manual workspace handoff.

Removing a saved meeting or call transcript also removes its recording after a clear confirmation. Cancel keeps both. Copies already exported or included in a handoff remain separate. Stopping or cancelling a recording in progress still keeps its recoverable audio.

Open Persona to choose artwork and show it over another app. Hide retains the prepared arrangement; End removes the live overlays. In Present, the Persona control sits beside the scene heading and the presentation actions stay visible below the preview.

Detect Meetings & Calls starts off. Enabling it may offer a recording when a known call app uses audio; it never records automatically. Choose the Mac app and optional microphone, then Start. A recording can run for up to two hours and keeps its original audio for recovery. Try your actual audio route before relying on it; a call remaining on your phone is not captured. Routed calls and headphone/two-sided audio remain unverified; the release notes list the other checks still open.
