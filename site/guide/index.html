Keep tools nearby

Click the Workbench icon in the Mac menu bar for quick actions. The floating toolbar keeps Dictate, Snap & Talk, Draw and Present within reach while another app is open.

The toolbar rests as a small indicator. Hover and it gently opens with quick actions; move away and it settles back. Click it to keep all tools open. Use Change mode to choose Original, Light or Natural dictation cleanup. The minus button returns to the quiet indicator and keeps it there until you hover again.

Drag the empty header space to move the toolbar, or choose a position from its menu. Menus stay open while you choose. When you record, the same panel keeps Stop visible; when you finish, it returns to your idle controls. Your chosen position and expanded preference are remembered. Reduce Motion in macOS makes disclosure immediate.

For keyboard access, choose Window → Focus floating toolbar, then press Space or Return to open its action menu. Use arrow keys to choose an action or change mode. Escape closes the menu; Escape again collapses the toolbar. Your configured shortcuts appear beside common actions.

  • Missing toolbar? Choose Window → Show floating toolbar in Workbench, or turn it on in Settings.
  • Missing menu-bar icon? Choose Window → Restore menu-bar icon. On a crowded Mac menu bar, the floating toolbar remains another way in.
  • Finished using the app window? Close it. Your tools keep running. Choose Quit Workbench to stop the app.

Dictate into a Mac text field

  1. Click where the words belong. Use your dictation shortcut or Dictate on the floating toolbar.
  2. Speak, then Stop. Expand the recording controls for details or Cancel.
  3. Check the result. Workbench tells you whether the words were pasted or copied. If the destination changed, paste the copied text where you want it.

Recent transcripts keeps the original and cleaned wording. Change shortcuts in Keyboard. If speech is not ready, open Models to complete setup.

Read something aloud

Open Read aloud, enter text and choose Listen. In apps that support macOS Services, select text and choose Services → Read Selection in Workbench. Review the imported text before listening.

Pause, resume or skip through the audio without generating it again. Mac voices work without an account. Speko is an optional online provider configured separately in Models.

Capture a screen and explain it

  1. Create a Snap & Talk session. Choose a local folder in the app once.
  2. Capture the next moment. Use Snap & Talk on the floating toolbar or its shortcut. Workbench captures the display under your pointer, then records your explanation.
  3. Stop, then continue. The screenshot and narration stay together while the next moment is captured.
  4. Review when ready. Edit titles and narration, reorder sections, or use Hand off to prepare a slide-deck prompt for your chosen AI app.

The toolbar hides during Workbench screen capture. Hand off copies a prompt and opens the chosen app; you decide when to share the session folder.

Prepare a scene, then present

  1. Add a scene. Choose a starter, your own backdrop or an editable scene copy.
  2. Make it yours. Double-click a title to rename it. Drag the device in the preview and add a customer logo if useful.
  3. Present. Choose a window for easy screen sharing, or full screen. Select the connected device when prompted.

In the scene list, use Command-click for separate items or Shift-click for a range. Delete or Backspace asks before removing the selected scenes. Drag rows to reorder; clear a search first. Return commits an inline name and Escape cancels it.

Workbench shows the phone's picture. Use the physical phone to tap, type and dictate. Connection and audio explains separate audio routes and Apple alternatives.

Click the small presentation control to change source or end. Escape closes open controls first, then ends the presentation. Ending a scene does not restore your desktop wallpaper.

Choose a logo and see motion

Use the logo controls to search the web inside Workbench. Choose an image, review it and use it in the current scene. A website page address may not be an image: use a direct image URL, copy an image, or choose a saved file when needed.

Animated starters preview their motion in the scene editor. Pause for precise positioning. Reduce Motion, disabled animated-image playback and power constraints can keep a preview still; the controls explain why.

PNG exports are still images. An animated desktop is a separate, explicit action; quitting stops its motion and leaves the still picture. More about backdrops · Ambient starters.

Draw or show an overlay card

Choose Draw to mark a detail on your screen. Escape returns to the app underneath; clearing your marks is a separate action. Use Screenshot when you want Apple's capture tool.

A scene keeps a backdrop, device and optional artwork together. An overlay card floats above an existing browser or app. Use overlay cards when the demo stays in that app; they are optional when preparing a device scene.

Choose Arrange overlays to prepare several cards. Keep them locked while presenting so clicks reach the app underneath. Overlay card guide.
