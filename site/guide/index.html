Welcome

You’re a minute away.

Workbench is made to be found by feel. Here’s the lay of the land and the few things worth trying first.

Set up

Three steps.

Free, with no account. The speech model downloads once, then works offline.

  1. Download Workbench and drag it into Applications.
  2. Open it. Home walks you through your first dictation.
  3. Allow each permission when a tool first asks. Nothing is requested up front.

For Macs with M1 or later, on macOS 14 or newer. The iPhone, iPad and Chrome editions are paused while the Mac app comes first.

Finding your way

Three ways in.

The same tools and saved work, wherever you start them.

  • The menu bar. Every tool, one row each, ready in a click.
  • The floating toolbar. Stays out of the way and becomes whatever you used last. Hover it for the next step.
  • The Workbench window. For preparing and reviewing: scenes, personas, History and Library.

Every tool has a keyboard shortcut. See them all, change them or practise them in Settings → Keyboard.

Try these first.

Dictate

Talk, and it types.

Click into any text field, start Dictate and speak. Your words land where you were working, and every transcript is kept in History. The live voice update adds words as you speak in supported text fields; other fields receive the completed text. Moving or editing the field stops live insertion.

Meetings

Your calls, written up.

Start on the detected call or pick its app. The live voice update shows words as the conversation happens, with Pause, Resume and Finish. Supported calls can finish after their audio ends and a cancellable countdown. Prepare follow-up opens a review for decisions and next steps. Nothing records until you press Start.

Read aloud

Hear what you wrote.

Paste a draft, or send selected text from any app, and listen with each word highlighted. Mac voices work offline.

Snap & Talk

Explain your screens. Get a deck.

Snap a few screens while you talk about them. Review, then hand the set to Claude or Codex to build the slides.

Draw

Point at anything.

Circle, arrow or sketch over whatever is on screen, then press Escape and carry on. Whiteboard and Timer live here too.

Persona

Put yourself on screen.

Your photo, a live camera bubble or a role card, floating over any app. Its ring can move as you talk.

History and Hand off

Nothing gets lost.

Dictations, meetings, snaps and decks in one searchable place. Select a few and hand them to an assistant; the result comes back here.

In Preview: review and reuse saved work

Review transcript shows the full current text and original wording without replacing your Dictate draft. Search can find words inside an assistant’s saved answer. Open Read result to copy it or choose Read aloud; an existing reading stays until you choose to replace it.

Team packs

The same kit for everyone.

In Library → Packs, paste the private repository your team shared and connect the GitHub account they invited. Your own copies never change when the pack updates.

Present

Put on a proper demo.

Pick a scene, plug in your iPhone (unlocked, trusting this Mac) and present in a window you can share in any call. The phone shows as video; you tap and talk on the phone itself.

Scenes and backdrops

Start from a starter or your own photo, add your logo, and drag the phone and backdrop into place. Scene options holds the rest: the device frame, logo, crop, device shape and hand. Every scene is a still picture you can also export.

Sharing your phone in a call

Plug your iPhone in with a data cable, unlock it and tap Trust. It appears in Present’s preview before you start; press Present and share the Workbench window in your call. If this Mac can’t see the phone, as some work Macs can’t, share straight from the Teams or Zoom app on the phone instead, or use Zoom’s cable share on the Mac to include the phone’s sound. Keep one device’s microphone and speaker on to avoid echo. In Present, Can’t see your phone? walks through the usual causes, checks the Mac with QuickTime Player and copies the details for IT. Sharing a browser tab shows only that tab. Rehearse once: talk, have someone remote reply, and try a reconnect.

Help

If something isn’t right.

  • Can’t see the toolbar or menu-bar icon? The Window menu brings both back.
  • Screen capture still fails after allowing it? Quit and reopen Workbench. A managed work Mac may block capture.
  • An update won’t install? Make sure Workbench is in Applications, not Downloads.
  • What changed? See what’s new in {{RELEASE_VERSION}}.
  • Still stuck? Choose Copy build details in Settings and tell us what happened.
