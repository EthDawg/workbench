Help shape Workbench

Like it? Make it better.

Workbench is free and open source, made by Ethan and Matt. If it has earned a place on your Mac, here are three easy ways to help. No code needed for the first one.

  • Tell us what you noticed
  • Hand it to your agent
  • Look under the hood

Feedback

Tell us what you noticed.

“I expected…” is enough. Describe the moment, what you expected and what happened. You don’t need to know why.

Your draft stays on this page until you copy it or open GitHub. Nothing is sent for you. Posting needs a GitHub account and is public, so use made-up examples.
