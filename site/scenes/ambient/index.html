Choose a place. Keep it yours.

Still posters from original generated artwork, not app screenshots or recorded nature videos. Workbench animates separate image details locally. These images alone cannot demonstrate playback.

Prefer your own photograph? Keep using it. Gentle photo motion adds the existing slow zoom, or leave it completely still. The new starters use localized motion instead; your device frame, logo and persona stay separate.

GET STARTED

A picture first. Your choice of use.

Mac: Open Present a device → Choose a starter. The three ambient starters begin with Device frame off. Use Present in window to preview movement, or More → Use as animated desktop. Enable Device frame when preparing a mobile demonstration.

iPhone or iPad: Tools → Scenes → Start with a picture. Choose a starter; Pause preview is beside Gentle motion. Saving an exported PNG to Photos gives you a still for Apple's Wallpaper picker.

These instructions describe the current source preview. Both apps need the new version to exchange an editable layered scene.

How this differs from Apple's wallpaper effects

Mac Dynamic wallpapers adjust with the time of day; aerials animate around lock and unlock. Neither is the same as Workbench drawing a local animated layer while it runs. Apple's Mac wallpaper guide.

On iPhone, eligible Live Photos can play on wake. iPadOS 26 also supports eligible Live Photo wake playback. Supported devices can offer spatial depth for eligible pictures. These are Apple's effects, not exported Workbench cloud or leaf motion. They do not make Workbench animate behind the Home Screen. iPhone Live Photos · iPadOS 26 Lock Screen · iPhone spatial wallpaper.

Saving to Photos and installing a wallpaper are separate steps. Follow Apple's Photos → Share → Use as Wallpaper route. This preview exports a PNG, with no video or Live Photo export.

RESEARCH → THREE PRIORITIES

Good framing. Clear ownership. Quiet motion.

  1. 01 · THE PICTURE

    Make the crop work.

    A few distinct scenes, space for your content and a preview at the right shape. Preserve the original when you make changes.

  2. 02 · THE DESTINATION

    Make stopping obvious.

    Say what persists and what needs the app open. Respect your later wallpaper choices. Keep presentation and desktop controls independent.

  3. 03 · THE EXPERIENCE

    Let motion take a back seat.

    Silent by default, easy to pause and still while cropping. No daily notifications or public content feed.

YOUR SCENE, KEPT LOCALLY

Keep the pictures and the editable work.

Each scene owns its poster and motion images. It can be saved, reopened and exchanged as an editable scene file without an account. Personal iCloud sync is optional. Adding motion does not add a content subscription or another server.

File compatibility, originals and recovery

Layered scenes use a version 2 .workbenchscene package containing exactly the required images. The first local upgrade preserves the old library manifest before writing the new version. Earlier still scenes remain readable; older apps refuse the new format rather than silently discard its recipe. Update both apps before exchanging layered scenes.

Unknown versions or presets are kept intact and require an update. A backdrop replacement clears or replaces its motion recipe; ordinary layout and crop changes retain it. Original mobile-composition attachments remain separate and recoverable. There is no script, URL or downloaded effect code in the recipe.

The artwork was generated originally for Workbench; its source files and provenance are in the repository. The app does not bundle Apple aerials or competitor media. Asset provenance · Original-composition recovery.
