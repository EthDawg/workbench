PREPARE ONCE, USE DELIBERATELY

A small journey, with clear edges.

  1. 01 · IPHONE OR IPAD

    Make a scene

    Open Tools → Scenes. Choose a photo, use the camera or start with a supplied picture. Set the background crop, device position and optional artwork.

  2. 02 · YOUR LIBRARY

    Keep an editable copy

    Save on this device. Opt into personal iCloud sync for the same Apple Account, or explicitly export a scene file. The photo inbox is another source of pictures.

  3. 03 · MAC

    Present when ready

    Open the saved scene in Present a device, choose a local video source and present in a window or full screen. The meeting app decides what to share.

The mobile device frame prepares a layout for later Mac video. It does not remotely operate another app or stream the phone into a scene by itself. Selected-photo handoff remains a separate, smaller transfer.

OPTIONAL MOTION · IMPLEMENTED IN SOURCE

A little movement. A steady foreground.

Turn on Gentle motion for a slow zoom of your own photograph. The new ambient starters move only their cloud or leaf details. Your device, logo and persona stay still. Mac presentation controls include Pause/Play; the iPhone scene editor has a separate Pause preview button. Cropping and exported images stay still.

In the Mac version intended for direct download, open More beside the presentation buttons, then choose Use as animated desktop. This applies a still first, then runs a separate desktop layer while Workbench is open. Pause reveals the still; Stop or Quit removes the layer. Ending a presentation leaves this separate desktop session alone.

Checked in a locally installed signed Mac Preview; not yet in the public download. No automatic login restart, video downloads or continuously animated iPhone Home Screen. Low Power Mode, Reduce Motion, high temperature and hidden previews suppress motion. iPhone/iPad wallpaper export remains a still; Apple offers Spatial Scene or Live Photo effects where eligible.

See the motion study and implementation decisions

The app animates one cached photograph layer, rather than rendering new bitmaps every frame. It uses existing scene files and no new server. Implementation, primary sources and acceptance limits · Canonical desktop-motion contract · Capture provenance and pixel comparison.

A .workbenchscene file carries the layout and source images without iCloud. Import creates a separate scene. Photo arrivals feed the library by copying selected pictures; they do not control a scene.

While Workbench is active: enabled scene sync groups local edits after a short pause. Going into the background stops scheduled and active transfers; saved work waits for the next foreground refresh. If a transfer fails, return to the app or choose Refresh to retry. It does not keep retrying in the background.

Changes preserve your choices

Competing edits keep both versions for review. A late upload receipt cannot replace a newer local edit. Removing a scene hides its record and retains original assets for recovery; it does not delete the source photo.

Changing Apple Accounts stops the existing sync relationship until you deliberately reconnect. Turning sync off keeps downloaded work. Corrupt or newer library formats pause writes rather than reset the library.

Presentations stay predictable

Starting a presentation freezes its scene. A later edit or cloud arrival cannot rearrange the live view. Placed persona artwork is an owned copy, so changing its library card does not silently change the scene.

Old mobile compositions remain recoverable with their original layers, caption and editable ink. Converting one creates a new scene; its landscape preview can differ from the original wallpaper or collage.

The implementation boundary

SceneSyncKit supplies a versioned local manifest, content-hashed image assets, portable packages and revision merging to both native apps. A separate CloudKit adapter uses CKSyncEngine with its own automatic scheduling disabled. Workbench groups committed local edits after 800 ms without another edit; a foreground cycle checks the account, fetches and sends saved work, then persists receipts and engine state. Unchanged saves and receipts cannot start an upload loop. The 800 ms delay is local scheduling, not an arrival-time promise.

Scenes and photos use separate zones and checkpoints in the same private container. There is no team-sharing service, public scene link or background-arrival guarantee. Public-database role permissions are not the access model for this private library. Apple’s CloudKit design guide · Exact store, recovery and acceptance contract.

PREPARED ARTWORK, SMALL LIVE CONTROLS

Give the persona a place in the scene.

Import a finished transparent card or choose one of eight fictional starter portraits. Edit the suggested role label and card background with native controls. Prepare groups before presenting; live controls switch among prepared people without exposing unrelated group names.

On Mac, a persona can also float above a browser. Its artwork lock is separate from its controls. Named positions provide an alternative to dragging; drag guides show the eight available positions and emphasize the nearest target. A whole-screen share can include separate controls and overlays. Add the persona to the scene when sharing only that window.
