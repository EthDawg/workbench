Talk, and it types.

Speak a prompt, a note or a whole email into any app. Turn on Meetings and Workbench writes up your calls too. Speech recognition runs on your Mac, and your original words are always kept.

Snap & Talk

Talk through your screens. Get a deck.

Snap a few screens while you explain them. Workbench keeps each snap with what you said, then hands the set to Claude or Codex to build the deck. Every snap stays searchable, even the words inside the picture.

Draw

Point at anything.

Pen, arrows and shapes over whatever is on screen, in any app. Press Escape and you are back in your work. A whiteboard and a break timer are one click away.

What it asks permission for, and when

Nothing is requested at first launch; each permission is asked for when you first use what needs it. Microphone access is requested when you first dictate, record a meeting or turn on React to my voice; Snap & Talk asks for it, with Screen Recording, when you open a session. Accessibility is optional, for automatic paste. Snap and Snap & Talk need Screen Recording access. Transcribing a call app needs Audio Recording access and macOS 14.2 or later. Camera access is used when you take a profile photo, start Persona’s camera bubble or choose device video. Drawing overlays alone need no permission.

This download is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Release notes and testing details · Download checksum. If macOS reports a damaged or unsafe app, stop and report the exact message. Apple’s opening guidance explains the normal checks. A managed work Mac may need your IT team’s approval.

Updates and your saved work

{{UPDATE_INSTALL_NOTE}}

{{UPDATE_DETAILS}} Saved text, scenes and settings live outside the application and survive replacement. To install a download over an existing copy, quit Workbench and replace it in the same Applications folder. If an update stopped because the app is in Downloads, quit Workbench, move {{APP_BUNDLE}} into Applications, open it from there and try again.

{{RELEASE_INSTALL_CONTEXT}} {{RELEASE_DATA_NOTE}} This edition stores its saved work under ~/Library/Application Support/{{APP_DATA_DIRECTORY}}.

Built-in speech runs locally after model download. A local server may forward audio if you configure it that way. Optional Speko reading sends your selected text to its service. See privacy details.

Which Macs it runs on

Workbench needs a Mac with an Apple M-series chip running macOS 14 or later. Check Apple menu → About This Mac. Intel Macs and Windows are not supported. Work on iPhone, iPad and the Chrome extension is paused while the Mac app comes first.
