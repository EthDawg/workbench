Four useful starting points.

Download for Mac ↓
  1. Quit Workbench and any older Voice or StageMark app. Unzip Workbench Preview.app. If Preview is already installed, replace it in the same Applications folder. For a first install, move it to your home folder’s Applications folder (~/Applications); use Finder’s Go → Home and create that folder if needed.
  2. Open Workbench and choose Dictate, Read aloud, Annotate or Present a device. The built-in speech model prepares on first launch; its download can take a few minutes.
  3. Try Keyboard to see your keys, change a combination and practise without activating a tool.

Your earlier Voice and StageMark data stays in place. Workbench imports a separate copy for this Preview. {{UPDATE_INSTALL_NOTE}}

Download checksum

Want to introduce yourself? Inside Workbench, “Say hello to Ethan and Matt” opens an editable draft in your email app. It is optional, and you choose whether to send.

Connect dictation to Apple Shortcuts

Add Record Audio → Transcribe with Workbench → Create Note, or another text action. Apple owns recording and its Stop button. Workbench uses your selected speech engine and returns text.

Changing a model does not require rebuilding your workflow. Some local servers choose their model at startup; check your server’s instructions.

Share a mobile demo

Open Present a device for saved scenes and a USB device presentation. A USB iPhone must be connected, unlocked and trusted. Availability depends on the device and macOS.

iPhone Mirroring opens Apple’s separate app and uses a nearby locked iPhone on supported systems. Share that app window directly in your meeting. QuickTime also opens separately. Workbench does not embed or control either Apple app.

Opening your download

Use the linked GitHub Preview release and its checksum. Read its signing, notarisation and testing notes before opening.

Microphone access is requested when you start dictation. Accessibility is optional for automatic paste. Camera access is used only when you choose device capture. Drawing overlays alone do not need Screen Recording access.

If macOS reports a damaged or unsafe app, stop and report the exact message. Apple’s opening guidance explains the normal checks. A managed work Mac may need your IT team’s approval.

Updates and your data

{{UPDATE_DETAILS}} Saved text, scenes and settings live outside the application and survive replacement.

After checking that your saved work is available in Workbench Preview, you can remove the older Voice and StageMark apps from Applications. Keep their saved-data folders. Removing an app does not remove its data; this Preview stores its copy under ~/Library/Application Support/Workbench Preview.

Built-in speech runs locally after model download. A local server may forward audio if you configure it that way. Optional Speko reading sends your selected text to its service. See privacy details.
