First time here?

  1. Unzip your download. Open the downloaded ZIP to unpack {{APP_BUNDLE}}.
  2. Give the app a home. Quit Workbench and any older Voice or StageMark app. Move the app into your home folder’s Applications folder (~/Applications). In Finder, choose Go → Home and create Applications if needed. If this edition is already installed, replace it in the same Applications folder, including /Applications if that is where it lives.
  3. Open it from Applications. Double-click that copy, then try Dictate with a thought you want to keep. The built-in speech model prepares on first launch; its download can take a few minutes.

Keep Workbench in Applications so future updates can finish. If you opened the copy in Downloads, quit it and relaunch the Applications copy.

{{UPDATE_INSTALL_NOTE}}

Make it yours in Settings → Keyboard: see your shortcuts and practise them. Inside Workbench, Say hello to Ethan and Matt opens an optional email draft for you to review and send.

Connect dictation to Apple Shortcuts

Add Record Audio → Transcribe with Workbench → Create Note, or another text action. Apple owns recording and its Stop button. Workbench uses your selected speech engine and returns text.

Changing a model does not require rebuilding your workflow. Some local servers choose their model at startup; check your server’s instructions.

Share a mobile demo

Open Present for saved scenes and a USB device presentation. A USB iPhone must be connected, unlocked and trusted. Availability depends on the device and macOS.

iPhone Mirroring opens Apple’s separate app and uses a nearby locked iPhone on supported systems. Share that app window directly in your meeting. QuickTime also opens separately. Workbench does not embed or control either Apple app.

Update stopped because the app is in Downloads?

Workbench can’t update while it is running from the location it was downloaded to. Quit Workbench, move {{APP_BUNDLE}} into Applications, then open it from there and try the update again.

If you already have this edition installed, replace it in the same Applications folder and open that copy. Keep your saved-data folders. Once you have checked your work, you can remove the spare extracted app from Downloads.

First-open permissions and Mac checks

This download is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple. Release notes and testing details · Download checksum

Nothing is requested at first launch; each permission is asked for when you first use what needs it. Microphone access is requested when you start dictation, narration or a meeting. Accessibility is optional, for automatic paste. Snap and Snap & Talk need Screen Recording access. Transcribing a call app needs Audio Recording access and macOS 14.2 or later. Camera access is used when you take a profile photo, start Persona’s camera bubble or choose device video. Drawing overlays alone need no permission.

If macOS reports a damaged or unsafe app, stop and report the exact message. Apple’s opening guidance explains the normal checks. A managed work Mac may need your IT team’s approval.
